# Hardware parody reference

Afterlight uses an original, compressed 32-year industry timeline. These are fictional names and original vector drawings inspired by recognizable real hardware generations. Dates, audiences, market shares, licensing fees, demand, and retirement windows are game-balancing values, not historical claims. No real hardware logos or product artwork are included.

The timeline includes different hardware formats rather than treating every release as the same market multiplier: home computers, cartridges, monochrome/color handhelds, optical discs, analog 3D consoles, online systems, dual screens, widescreen portables, motion controls, hybrids, and fast-storage consoles.

Primary research references: [Nintendo history](https://www.nintendo.com/au/about/history/), [Nintendo company history](https://press.nintendo.com/CompanyHistory), [PlayStation history](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/playstation-history/1994-ps-one/), and [Xbox history documentary announcement](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2021/12/13/xbox-docuseries-power-on-release/amp/).
